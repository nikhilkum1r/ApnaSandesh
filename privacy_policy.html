1. Zero-Data-Collection Architecture (No Cloud Servers)

Unlike cloud-based CRM and bulk messaging platforms that store your address book on remote servers:

  • No Cloud Servers: Trustoryx Digital does not host or operate remote databases, API gateways, or telemetry collection servers that receive or process your business data.
  • Absence of Internet Permission: The application manifest completely omits android.permission.INTERNET. The Android OS kernel guarantees that no packet can be transmitted off your phone.
  • App-Private SQLite Sandbox: All business profiles, client records, custom tags, and campaign dispatches reside in the sandboxed local SQLite database (/data/data/com.apnasandesh.app/databases/biz_sms_database), inaccessible to other apps.
  • Excluded from Cloud Backups: The database is explicitly excluded from unencrypted Google Drive device backups via Android's data_extraction_rules.xml and backup_rules.xml.

2. Android Operating System Permissions Specification

Google Play Compliant Release (v1.0.2+): To strictly comply with Google Play's SMS and Call Log policy, the Google Play version of ApnaSandesh operates exclusively via the zero-permission Android SMS Composer Mode (Intent.ACTION_SENDTO). SEND_SMS, READ_PHONE_STATE, and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE are omitted from the Play Store manifest. Only READ_CONTACTS (optional customer import) and POST_NOTIFICATIONS (local alerts) are declared.

In strict accordance with the Google Play Sensitive Permissions Policy, every runtime permission requested by ApnaSandesh is documented below with its exact purpose and zero-permission fallback:

android.permission.SEND_SMS Optional / Runtime

Core Functionality: Dispatches sequential SMS campaign messages directly via the phone's cellular SIM card using Android's native SmsManager when the user enables "Direct SIM Sending".

Restrictions & User Control:

  • Messages are sent only upon explicit user initiation for a campaign or client message.
  • Zero silent, covert, background, or premium SMS messages are ever initiated.
  • Live interactive preview calculates exact character counts, GSM-7 / Unicode encoding, and SMS segments before dispatch.

Zero-Permission Alternative: When Direct SIM mode is disabled, the app uses the Android system SMS composer (COMPOSER_HANDOFF) to draft messages in your default SMS app with zero SMS permissions.

No Inbox Access: We do not declare READ_SMS or RECEIVE_SMS. The app has zero access to your incoming text messages, personal conversations, or OTP codes.

android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE Optional / Runtime

Purpose: On Dual-SIM devices, queries active SIM subscription IDs via SubscriptionManager so the business owner can select which cellular subscription (e.g., SIM 1 vs SIM 2) to use for sending.

Strict Non-Collection: We do not collect or read IMEI, IMSI, device phone numbers, call state, call logs (READ_CALL_LOG is not declared), or cellular traffic metadata.

android.permission.READ_CONTACTS Optional / Runtime

Purpose: Used only when the user taps "Browse All Device Contacts" to select and import client records from the local address book into the local customer database.

Data Handling: Contact records are loaded into memory and written directly to the local SQLite database. They are never transmitted over the internet or shared with third parties.

Zero-Permission Alternatives: Users can import contacts one-by-one using the Android system contact picker (PickContact) or via CSV spreadsheet files without granting any contact permissions.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) Optional / Runtime

Purpose: Displays active campaign dispatch progress (e.g., "Sending 12/50...") with interactive Pause and Stop controls in the notification drawer.

Non-Marketing: No promotional push messages or remote notifications (e.g., FCM) exist in the application.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE & SPECIAL_USE (Android 14+) Foreground Policy

Declared Subtype: Autonomous campaign SMS message dispatching and progress tracking

Purpose: Keeps active sequential cellular dispatch jobs uninterrupted by Android Doze mode or aggressive battery management when the user locks their screen or switches apps during a multi-message batch.

3. Third-Party Services, SDKs & Trackers

To preserve complete data confidentiality:

  • Zero Analytics SDKs: No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, Adjust, or Mixpanel.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: No AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, or monetization SDKs.

4. Data Retention & Mandatory Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion guidelines, users maintain complete and unconditional control over their data:

Data Retention

All client profiles, message drafts, delivery audit receipts, and suppression entries remain on your device for as long as you keep the application installed, or until you choose to modify or purge them.

How to Delete Your Data (Google Play Requirement)

Because ApnaSandesh maintains no remote servers or online user accounts, data deletion is 100% self-serve, instantaneous, and complete:

1

In-App Complete Wipe

Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Permanently Delete All Data. This immediately truncates and recreates all database tables on your phone.

2

Android Settings Wipe

Go to Settings → Apps → ApnaSandesh → Storage → Clear Storage to immediately wipe all private application sandbox data.

3

App Uninstallation

Uninstalling the application causes the Android operating system to immediately and irreversibly delete all internal database files and preferences.

5. Customer Privacy, Opt-Outs & Telecom Compliance

The application provides small business owners with built-in compliance safeguards:

  • On-Device Suppression List: Clients who text "STOP" or request opt-out are recorded locally as suppressed. The app automatically blocks sending to these numbers in all future dispatches.
  • Commercial Hours Guidance: Visual advisories align campaigns with commercial transmission rules (09:00 to 21:00 local time) in compliance with TRAI (India) and TCPA (USA).
  • Local Audit Receipts: Timestamped delivery receipts (Sent, Delivered, Failed) are retained strictly in the local SQLite database for compliance verification.
